Setup Guide
Windows PC
Highlight any text in any app and press Ctrl + Alt + C. It sends immediately to your phone.
Android
1. In Chrome, tap menu (⋮) > "Add to Home screen".
2. Highlight text in any app, tap Share > select ClipFlow.
iPhone (iOS)
1. In Safari, tap Share > "Add to Home Screen".
2. For 1-tap share, add a Shortcut with action "Get Contents of URL" pointing to http://.../api/send (POST JSON with key text).
